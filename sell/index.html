HomeSell on NearBuy

Your counter, on the internet.

NearBuy does not replace the shopkeeper. It gives you a counter that behaves like the one you already run: your catalogue, your stock, your prices, your hours — with discovery, delivery coordination and settlement added around it.

What you get

Everything a single shop cannot afford alone.

Discovery, ordering, payment records, delivery coordination and an operations console — without building any of it yourself.

Catalogue & stock that stay true

Create products with price, pack size, tax and return window. Stock changes are atomic, and reserved quantities come off the shelf the moment an order is placed.

An order queue, not an inbox

Accept, reject with a reason, pack against the list, mark ready. The customer sees every transition, so the "where is my order" call mostly stops happening.

Delivery you can hand over

Request a nearby Runner, deliver it yourself, or offer free store pickup. Handover works against a PIN so parcels do not disappear between counters.

Numbers you can act on

Today's orders, revenue, low-stock alerts, returns and a settlement statement with commission and refunds itemised per order.

Promotions you control

Run your own coupon with a minimum basket, a cap and an end date. It appears on the public offers page only while it is genuinely live.

Staff and hours

Add staff accounts with their own access, set business hours per day, and pause ordering when the counter is too busy.

Onboarding

Four steps from application to first order.

The whole flow is one wizard in the Seller Hub. You can stop after step one and come back — your account and draft shop stay saved.

01

Create the account

Owner name, email, mobile and a password. This is a seller account, separate from any customer account you may have.

02

Describe the business

Registered or trading name, shop type, category, full address and contact details for the counter.

03

Submit documents

PAN, GST registration where applicable, and one address or identity proof. Document metadata is recorded against your application.

04

Get reviewed

Operations approves or returns the application with a reason. Approved shops unlock the full hub and appear in the public directory.

Ask a question
Do I need a registered company to sell?

No. Sole proprietors, partnerships and family-run shops are all welcome. We ask for PAN and, if you are GST-registered, your GSTIN. Home-based and market-stall businesses are supported as shop types.

What does NearBuy charge?

There is no listing fee. Commission applies only to completed orders, at the rate agreed in writing during onboarding, and every order line on your settlement statement shows the commission applied.

Can I keep offering store pickup?

Yes. Pickup is set per shop, and you can charge for it or keep it free. Customers collect against a collection code that you mark complete at the counter.

Who handles delivery?

You choose per order: request a nearby Runner, hand it to your own staff, or let the customer collect. Runner jobs are ranked by distance and workload and expire if nobody accepts.

What happens if an item is out of stock?

Stock is reserved at checkout, but if a count is wrong you can pack a shortfall with a reason. The customer is told what changed and the line total adjusts before handover.

How do I get paid?

Cash-on-delivery and pay-at-store orders settle to your recorded balance. Settlement statements list gross, refunds, commission and net per period. Bank transfers require a payout provider, which this deployment does not have connected.
