What is not here

Honest gaps, in one place.

These are provider integrations this deployment does not have. None of them are simulated in the interface.

  • Online card and UPI payments — enabled only when gateway keys are configured; cash on delivery and pay-at-store work today.
  • SMS and email — no provider connected, so verification codes are shown on screen in development and no message is sent.
  • Google sign-in — shown with an explanation on the sign-in screen, not enabled.
  • Map tiles and routing — distances are straight-line estimates multiplied by a road factor, stated in the UI.
  • Payout transfers — earnings and requests are recorded; no money moves automatically.
  • Real photography per SKU — seeded products use labelled emoji tiles where no photo exists.
