Your neighbourhood, organised.

NearBuy connects the shops on your street with the people who live around them — and with the neighbours who deliver between the two. One shared data model sits behind a customer storefront, a seller hub, a delivery partner app, fleet coordination and an operations console.

The idea

Commerce that stays on your street.

Marketplaces optimise for the cheapest seller anywhere. NearBuy optimises for the nearest seller: the grocer who knows your order, the bakery that sells out by ten, the stationer two lanes over. Distance is the feature, not a constraint.

Every shop keeps its own catalogue, stock, timings and fulfilment preference. NearBuy adds the parts a single shop cannot afford alone — discovery, ordering, payments records, delivery coordination and a control tower for the network.

Nothing here replaces the shopkeeper. It gives them a counter on the internet that behaves like the one they already run.

How it works

One order, several neighbours.

1

Discover

Pick your neighbourhood and see only the shops that can actually serve you, with distance, timings and what they sell.

2

Order

Fill one basket across several shops, then choose home delivery or free pickup at checkout.

3

Fulfil

The shop accepts and packs; a nearby partner picks up with a pickup PIN and delivers with proof of delivery.

4

Settle

Delivery posts to a ledger, fees and GST are calculated per order, and the shop sees a settlement statement.

One backend

Three front doors.

Each portal signs in on its own and opens only its own tools. Landing on another portal shows that portal’s sign-in — a customer session never unlocks seller, partner or operations screens.

Trust & safety

The rules we hold ourselves to.

Sessions stay put

Each portal keeps its own session in the current tab and clears it on sign-out, so one login never opens another workspace.

Verification, not buttons

Age gates, document checks and pickup PINs are modelled as real steps — the demo simulates them rather than skipping them.

Nothing leaves your browser

Enquiries, orders and applications in this prototype are stored locally and labelled DEMO DATA. No email is sent and no one is paged.

Humans decide

Approvals, refunds and sensitive actions are described as staff decisions with reason codes and appeals — automation must never make irreversible calls.

Prototype notice. This is a simulated demo. Checkout charges nothing, no shop is contacted, no runner is dispatched, no OTP is sent, no application is really verified, no payout or bank account exists, and no personal or sensitive information should be entered. Maps, distances and pricing are schematic.
