Why local

Commerce that stays on your street.

Marketplaces optimise for the cheapest seller anywhere in the country. NearBuy optimises for the nearest seller: the grocer who knows your order, the bakery that sells out by ten, the stationer two lanes over.

  • The shop keeps its identity. Its own name, hours, prices and fulfilment rules — NearBuy adds discovery and delivery.
  • You can always see the shop behind the product. Every listing names its seller, distance and stock, never a faceless catalogue.
  • Delivery is a neighbour, not a depot. Runner jobs go to partners already inside your locality, ranked by distance and current workload.

From your street to your doorstep

Four steps, no surprises.

The same sequence runs for every order, whichever shop it comes from — and every step is a real server-side state, not a status label.

01

Discover

Share your pincode and see only the shops that can actually serve you, with distance, timings and live stock.

02

Order

Fill one basket across several shops and choose delivery or free pickup shop by shop at checkout.

03

Fulfil

The shop accepts and packs. A nearby Runner picks up against a handover PIN and delivers against your OTP.

04

Settle

Every order posts a receipt and a settlement line, so the shop can see exactly what it earned and when.
