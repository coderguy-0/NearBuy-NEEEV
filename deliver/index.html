Partner questions

The honest answers.

This is a demo deployment, and the payouts section says exactly what is real.

Is delivery work full-time?

No. You choose your hours by going online and offline in the portal. Many partners ride for two hours in the morning and two in the evening.

How are payouts calculated?

Each delivery offer carries a payout amount before you accept it, based on the distance and the order. Completed deliveries accumulate in your earnings view, and you can request a payout from the portal.

What if the customer is not answering?

Do not hand the parcel over without the OTP. Use the safety centre in the portal to raise an incident and operations will guide you, with the order and your job reference attached.

Can I deliver for my own shop?

If you run a shop on NearBuy, the Seller Hub lets you mark an order as self-delivered. That is separate from being a Runner partner, and it does not create a partner payout.

Are payouts actually transferred here?

No. This deployment records earnings and payout requests, but no payout provider is connected, so no money moves automatically. That limitation is stated in the portal too.
