Questions

Eight things people ask.

Need more? The in-app help centre goes deeper per workspace, or send us a question below.

What is NearBuy?

NearBuy is a neighbourhood commerce platform that connects customers with local shops and short-distance delivery partners. It brings nearby stores, everyday products and Runner delivery together in one place.

How do I find shops that deliver to me?

Open the customer storefront and choose your neighbourhood. Only shops that are serviceable to your address are shown, with distance, opening hours and what they sell.

Can I order from more than one shop at once?

Yes. Your basket holds items from several shops and splits into one order per shop at checkout, each with its own fulfilment method and tracking.

How do I register my shop?

Use the seller registration route at /seller/register/ to work through a three-step application: account, business details, then KYC documents. The Admin Portal reviews every application, and selling unlocks once your shop is approved.

How do I become a delivery partner?

Send the application form at /runner/register/. It is stored in the platform database and reviewed by operations; once approved you sign in to the Runner Portal, go online and control your own availability.

What does delivery cost?

Delivery fees follow the pricing bands set for your service area and are shown before you confirm. Shops may offer free delivery above a basket value — the storefront labels those offers.

How do refunds and returns work?

Raise an issue on an order from your account. The shop reviews it, can restock items and issue a refund; the ledger and settlement statement reflect the adjustment.

Is my data shared between portals?

No. Customer, seller, delivery-partner and administrator sessions are separate cookies and isolated on the server. Signing in to one portal never grants access to another.
