HomeHow NearBuy works

One order, several neighbours, no mystery.

NearBuy is a neighbourhood commerce platform: customers discover shops that can actually reach them, shops fulfil what they sell, and delivery partners carry the parcels in between. Here is exactly how the pieces fit.

Explore nearby shops Sell on NearBuy
The model

Distance is the feature, not a constraint.

Most marketplaces optimise for the cheapest seller anywhere. NearBuy optimises for the nearest seller who has the item in stock today — because a ten-minute walk beats a two-day wait for milk.

01 · Discover

The pincode decides

Each shop has coordinates, a delivery radius and a list of service pincodes. Enter yours and the directory hides anything that cannot reach you, rather than showing it greyed out.

02 · Basket

One bag, several shops

Add items from the grocer, the chemist and the baker in one basket. At checkout it splits into one order per shop, each with its own fulfilment method, fee and timeline.

03 · Fulfil

The shop packs, a neighbour delivers

The seller accepts or rejects, packs against the order, then either hands over to a Runner or completes a store pickup against the customer's collection code.

04 · Settle

Every rupee is traceable

Totals, discounts, tax and delivery fees are calculated on the server. Each order produces a customer receipt, a seller copy and a settlement line.

Runner jobs are offered to partners already inside the locality, ranked by distance and current workload.

Delivery & pickup

Two handovers, two codes.

Handover is where neighbourhood deliveries usually go wrong. NearBuy makes both ends explicit:

  • The pickup PIN. The seller sees it when the parcel is ready; the runner must present it before the job can be marked picked up.
  • The delivery OTP. Derived on the server and revealed to the customer only once the parcel is out for delivery. Never stored in plain text.
  • One job at a time. A runner cannot accept a second job while one is active, and assignment is a single conditional update, so two partners can never own the same parcel.
  • Offers expire. Unaccepted offers lapse after the configured window and the seller can re-request, or deliver it themselves.
Pricing

Who pays what.

Nothing is calculated in your browser. The bag, the fees and the totals are assembled on the server, so a tampered page cannot change a price.

Customers

Pay the item price, any delivery fee the shop has set (often waived above a basket threshold) and applicable tax. Store pickup is free where a shop offers it.

Shops

Keep their own selling price and set their own delivery fee, minimum basket and free-delivery threshold. The settlement statement lists commission per order where a rate applies.

Delivery partners

See the payout and distance before accepting an offer. Payouts are recorded against each delivered job and appear in the earnings view of the Runner Portal.

Online card and UPI payments are enabled only when a payment gateway is configured for the deployment. Until then, cash on delivery and pay-at-store are the working methods, and the checkout says so plainly.

The status timeline

What each status actually means.

Placed

Stock is reserved for every line, all lines or none.

Accepted

The shop confirmed it can fulfil the order.

Preparing

Items are being picked and packed against the list.

Ready

Packed and waiting for a runner or customer collection.

Picked up

The runner presented the shop's handover PIN.

Out for delivery

Your delivery OTP becomes visible at this point.

Delivered

The runner entered your OTP. The job is complete.

Completed

A pickup order collected against the collection code.

Rejections, cancellations and returns each carry a reason and release reserved stock back to the shop.

Full help centre
How does delivery work?

Each shop fulfils its own part of your basket. Choose home delivery or store pickup per shop at checkout. Delivery fees, minimum order values and estimated times are set by each seller and shown before you pay.

Can I order from more than one shop?

Yes. Your basket is grouped by shop and becomes one parent order with a separate order for each shop. Sellers only ever see their own items and only ever receive their own share of the payment.

How do I know my order is nearby?

Open Orders → your order. Once a seller accepts and prepares it, the status timeline updates as the order is picked up and sent out for delivery. If a NearBuy runner is assigned, their name and status appear on the order page.

What is the delivery OTP for?

For home delivery, the runner must enter the 6-digit code shown on your order page before the order can be marked delivered. Never share the code before you have the items.

How does store pickup work?

Choose Store pickup at checkout for a shop that offers it. You will get a collection code on your order page. Show it at the counter; the shop marks the order complete once it is handed over.

Which payment methods can I use?

Cash on delivery and pay at store work today. UPI, cards, net banking and wallets are enabled as soon as a payment gateway is configured for the deployment — the checkout says clearly when online payment is unavailable.
